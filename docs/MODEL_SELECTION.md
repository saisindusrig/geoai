## FAST decision: REJECT FAST_MODEL FOR NOW

The small model produced valid final structured output in 2/3 cases. Scores were 70, 100 and 0; it invoked unadvertised site tools in the explanation case and failed the selection-case schema on both initial response and repair. The bridge recognition was correct but declared PROPOSAL_ONLY instead of the case's read-only recognition scope. Its final explanation also overstated unavailable generation/analysis/approval as merely dependent on missing site data. These correctness failures override its lower latency and token usage. `NEBIUS_FAST_MODEL` remains blank; all current Assistant traffic routes to provisional PRIMARY.

Large-Qwen scores were 0, 90 and 90, also 2/3 valid. Its explanation response and repair truncated, its bridge clarification included unevidenced hypothetical Road A/River B labels, and selection classification was SITE_QUERY rather than expected QUESTION. Selection identities/tool use were correct. PRIMARY remains the user's explicit provisional choice, with these new observations added to the unresolved findings. No cases were rerun or prompts tuned after results.
